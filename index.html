Case Study / I.

Systems behind
the storefront.

A full-stack e-commerce platform for health & wellness products—React/TypeScript storefront, Node.js/Express API, Prisma + PostgreSQL, JWT authentication and Redis caching.

Role

Freelance Full-Stack Developer

Focus

APIs, Auth, Caching

Stack

React, TypeScript, Node.js, Express, Prisma, PostgreSQL, Redis

The challenge

Product pages buckled under peak traffic—every load hammered the database with identical queries and no caching layer existed to absorb it. I introduced shared Redis caching with graceful DB fallback, eliminating redundant queries on the hot path.
