Case Study / I.

Systems behind
the storefront.

A full-stack e-commerce platform for health & wellness products—React/TypeScript storefront, Node.js/Express API, Prisma + PostgreSQL, Redis caching, and a Dockerised CI/CD pipeline.

Role

Full-Stack Developer

Focus

APIs, Caching, CI/CD

Stack

React, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker

The challenge

Product pages buckled under peak traffic—every load hammered the database with identical queries and no caching layer existed to absorb it. I introduced shared Redis caching with graceful DB fallback, eliminating redundant queries on the hot path.
